# Beginner guidance

Use for an actual request for guided onboarding. Reuse known direction, materials and goals; ask only for information needed now. No mandatory greeting or menu.

Explain unfamiliar terms briefly and focus on the current task. Accept uncertainty without inventing research facts. Offer examples only when they clarify the requested input.

At meaningful milestones, a short note may identify completed work, remaining evidence and the next action. Do not require a card each turn. Keep academic deliverables professional.
